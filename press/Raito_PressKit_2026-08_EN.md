### Angle B — One track, 20 million Spotify streams
"Gathers Under Night...", the character select theme from *UNDER NIGHT IN-BIRTH*, has passed **20.2 million streams on Spotify alone** (verified August 2026). Game BGM circulating as standalone music, at that scale, is the story. Covered by Noisy Pixel on August 5, 2026.

### Angle C — "Game music that goes beyond the game"
This is Raito's stated design philosophy, in his own words. Angle B is what happens when it works. A natural fit for a craft or creator-focused interview.

### Angle D — What does a composer sell in the age of generative AI?
Raito publicly states that he **accepts commissions by direct request only — no competitive bidding, no pitch competitions** — and has raised his rates. Few working composers will discuss the economics of AI-era music production on the record, with actual numbers.

### Angle E — A global career run from Okinawa
Raito has kept his company in Naha, Okinawa since 2010, winning three regional advertising awards while scoring globally released titles.

## 5. Biography (three lengths)

### Short (approx. 40 words)
Raito is a Japanese composer known for fighting game music, including the *MELTY BLOOD* and *UNDER NIGHT IN-BIRTH* series. He is the CEO of Lisa-Rec Inc. and is based in Okinawa.

### Medium (approx. 100 words)
Raito (来兎) began composing game music in 1997. He has written for the *MELTY BLOOD* series since 2003 and for the *UNDER NIGHT IN-BIRTH* series since 2012, handling both music and sound effects through *II Sys:Celes* (2024). His work outside fighting games includes the soundtrack for the TV anime *SK8 the Infinity*, arrangements for *Fate/Grand Order*, sound effects for Lab Zero Games' *Indivisible*, and composition and arrangement for *Double Dragon Revive* (2025). He founded Lisa-Rec Inc. in Okinawa in 2010. His catalog has passed 100 million streams.

### Long (approx. 250 words)
Raito (来兎) is a Japanese composer and sound designer, born in 1976, who has been writing game music since 1997 and went independent from French-Bread in 2003.

Fighting games are the spine of his career. He has scored the *MELTY BLOOD* series since the 2003 original — a run that reaches its latest entry, *MELTY BLOOD: TWI-LUMINA*, due in early 2027 — and has handled music and sound effects for the *UNDER NIGHT IN-BIRTH* series from its 2012 debut through *II Sys:Celes* in 2024. Two decades with two series is a run few composers in the genre can match.

His work extends well past fighting games: the original soundtrack for the TV anime *SK8 the Infinity* (2021), arrangement work on *Fate/Grand Order Original Soundtrack V*, theme songs for the anime *We Never Learn*, sound effects for *Indivisible* by Lab Zero Games in the US, and both composition and arrangement for Arc System Works' *Double Dragon Revive*, released in 2025.

In March 2010 he founded Lisa-Rec Inc. in Naha, Okinawa, where he serves as CEO, producing advertising music, sound logos and music for commercial spaces. He has won bronze at the Okinawa Advertising Awards three times, applying the short-form craft of looping game music to fifteen-second commercials.

His stated aim is "game music that goes beyond the game." By that measure it is working: his catalog has passed 100 million streams, one *UNDER NIGHT IN-BIRTH* track alone accounts for more than 20 million of them, and he draws over 600,000 monthly listeners on Spotify. In 2024 he moved the catalog of his own label, lisa-rec, to the Swedish label SNAFU. He still works from Okinawa.

## 8. Quotes

> ⚠️ **Drafted from the artist's stated positions. Please clear with the artist before publication.**

> "I'm after game music that goes beyond the game. Music you'd want to hear when you're not playing. That's what widens what game music can be."

> "Twenty million plays means it reached people who never played the game. You don't get to that number from inside a game."

> "I've been writing *MELTY BLOOD* since 2003 and *UNDER NIGHT IN-BIRTH* since 2012. When you stay with a series that long, you become the keeper of how it sounds. Doing something new without betraying that memory — that's the hard part."

> "When AI can generate an infinite supply of average music, the only thing a composer sells is 'I want *you* to do it.' That's why I don't take competitive bids. Fewer jobs, done deeper."

> "I've never felt that being in Okinawa was a disadvantage. Sound travels anywhere."

## 10. FAQ

**How should the name be written?**
**Raito** in English; 来兎 in Japanese. Some releases credit **RAITO** in all caps. Both refer to the same person.

**Can the legal name be published?** Yes — Masaru Kuba.

**Can his age be published?** Yes — born January 30, 1976 (age 50 as of August 2026).

**How do Raito and Lisa-Rec Inc. relate?** Raito is the artist name; Lisa-Rec Inc. is the music production company he founded and runs. Game and anime work is handled under his own name (raito.studio); domestic Japanese advertising and commercial-space work goes through Lisa-Rec (lisa-rec.net).

**Are English-language interviews possible?** Yes. He has worked with overseas developers and publishers.

**Remote interviews?** Yes. He is based in Naha, Okinawa.

**Licensing music for a program or article?** Rights differ by title; publishers or labels may hold the master rights. Please start at contact@raito.studio.
