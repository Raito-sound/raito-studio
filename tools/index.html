RAITO STUDIO TOOLS

Tools for writing. Tools for music.

Free browser tools by composer Raito: TALLY for Japanese lyrics and text, CARVE for audio editing, KEYS for piano and recording, and PITCH for tuning instruments.

Open a tool in your browser. Your writing, performances and input audio are processed on your device.

About Raito, composer and sound designer ↗

TALLY

Write Japanese text. Count characters and the sounds in your lyrics.

A free browser text editor for Japanese writing, character counts and kana-based mora analysis. Check lyrics line by line and export TXT files.

Features & how to use ↗Open app ↗

CARVE

Trim audio, shape fades and listen closely to your loop seams.

Edit audio in your browser for free. Trim selections, adjust fades and gain, check loop seams, and export WAV, OGG Vorbis or MP3 with CARVE.

Features & how to use ↗Open app ↗

KEYS

Play a phrase. Practice it. Keep the idea.

A free browser piano and organ with a metronome and phrase recording. Play with your computer keyboard or touch, then export MIDI or WAV.

Features & how to use ↗Open app ↗

PITCH

A clear meter for tuning guitar, bass and more.

Tune guitar, bass, ukulele and strings in your browser. PITCH offers a large cents meter, tuning presets, a visual strobe and reference tones for free.
