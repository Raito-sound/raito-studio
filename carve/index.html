CARVE

Shape your sound.
Finish the loop.

A wave editor that runs in your browser. Open an audio file, select a range and get to work—from fades and level adjustments to game-music loop checks.

Edit on your own device, then export WAV, OGG or MP3. No installation or account needed.

FEATURES

Get closer
to the waveform.

Edit a selection
Delete, copy, insert-paste or extract a selection into a new file. Snap selection edges to zero crossings to fine-tune your edit points.
Shape fades and levels
Choose from five fade-in and fade-out curves. Normalize to a target peak level or adjust gain by a specific number of decibels.
Listen to the loop seam
Use Seam to audition the transition from the loop end back to its start. Set markers to export the loop region as LOOPSTART and LOOPLENGTH tags in an OGG file.
Export in the format you need
Export WAV at 16-bit, 24-bit or 32-bit float, or choose OGG Vorbis or MP3. Imports use the original sample rate and show a warning if it cannot be preserved.

QUICK START

Open a file.
Select a range.

  1. Open CARVE, then drop an audio file such as WAV, AIFF, FLAC or MP3 into the editor, or select it with Open.
  2. Drag across the waveform to select a range. Adjust fades, levels or loop points.
  3. Choose an output format and click Export to save your audio file.

Audio is not uploaded to a server. OGG and MP3 export need a network connection to load the encoder. A desktop or laptop is recommended for precise waveform editing.
