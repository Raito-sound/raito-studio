03 / PROFILE

A long game,
played from Okinawa.

Raito (来兎) is a Japanese composer and sound designer based in Naha, Okinawa. He began writing game music in 1997 and is best known for the MELTY BLOOD and UNDER NIGHT IN-BIRTH series.

His work also includes Double Dragon Revive, the in-show song for the TV anime SK8 the Infinity, arrangements for Fate/Grand Order, and sound effects for Indivisible.

He founded Lisa-Rec Inc. in 2010 and continues to work from Okinawa, where he also composes all of the company’s commercial music and sound logos.
