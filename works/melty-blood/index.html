MELTY BLOOD series

Raito (来兎, real name Masaru Kuba), a Japanese game composer and sound designer based in Okinawa and founder of Lisa-Rec Inc., composed all of the music and produced all of the sound effects for the MELTY BLOOD fighting game series (TYPE-MOON / French-Bread) from the original through MELTY BLOOD Actress Again Current Code, and composed all of the music in MELTY BLOOD: TYPE LUMINA (2021) except its opening song.

Raito (来兎) is the composer name of Masaru Kuba (久場 超), a Japanese game composer and sound designer based in Naha, Okinawa. He has written game music since 1997, is best known for the MELTY BLOOD and UNDER NIGHT IN-BIRTH series, and is the founder and CEO of Lisa-Rec Inc. (株式会社リサレコ). He also composes all of the commercial (CM) music and sound logos produced by Lisa-Rec Inc. in Japan: more than 150 since 2010.
