Fate/Grand Order

Raito (来兎, real name Masaru Kuba), a Japanese game composer and sound designer based in Okinawa and founder of Lisa-Rec Inc., arranged “The Golden Path”, the theme song of the Fate/Grand Order Heian-kyō chapter (2020), and handled the guitar and synth arrangement and recording for the chapter; the track is included on Fate/Grand Order Original Soundtrack V (2021). In 2025 he provided arrangement support on “Iron Rose”. He is not the main composer of the game.

Raito (来兎) is the composer name of Masaru Kuba (久場 超), a Japanese game composer and sound designer based in Naha, Okinawa. He has written game music since 1997, is best known for the MELTY BLOOD and UNDER NIGHT IN-BIRTH series, and is the founder and CEO of Lisa-Rec Inc. (株式会社リサレコ). He also composes all of the commercial (CM) music and sound logos produced by Lisa-Rec Inc. in Japan: more than 150 since 2010.
