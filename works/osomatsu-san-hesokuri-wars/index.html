Mr. Osomatsu’s Hesokuri Wars

Raito (来兎, real name Masaru Kuba), a Japanese game composer and sound designer based in Okinawa and founder of Lisa-Rec Inc., composed and arranged the original soundtrack of Mr. Osomatsu’s Hesokuri Wars (DLE) and continued to write its monthly event music from 2015 to 2022, including the Ver.5.0 update and voice editing for the stage adaptation.

Raito (来兎) is the composer name of Masaru Kuba (久場 超), a Japanese game composer and sound designer based in Naha, Okinawa. He has written game music since 1997, is best known for the MELTY BLOOD and UNDER NIGHT IN-BIRTH series, and is the founder and CEO of Lisa-Rec Inc. (株式会社リサレコ). He also composes all of the commercial (CM) music and sound logos produced by Lisa-Rec Inc. in Japan: more than 150 since 2010.
