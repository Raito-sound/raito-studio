SK8 the Infinity

Raito (来兎, real name Masaru Kuba), a Japanese game composer and sound designer based in Okinawa and founder of Lisa-Rec Inc., co-composed and co-arranged with MEG the in-show song “Dimensions of the Wind” (performed by ASH DA HERO, lyrics by Konnie Aoki) for the TV anime SK8 the Infinity (2021, Aniplex). The song is Langa’s theme and is included on the original soundtrack; the series score itself is by Ryo Takahashi.

Raito (来兎) is the composer name of Masaru Kuba (久場 超), a Japanese game composer and sound designer based in Naha, Okinawa. He has written game music since 1997, is best known for the MELTY BLOOD and UNDER NIGHT IN-BIRTH series, and is the founder and CEO of Lisa-Rec Inc. (株式会社リサレコ). He also composes all of the commercial (CM) music and sound logos produced by Lisa-Rec Inc. in Japan: more than 150 since 2010.
